What to Expect at
Aapa Cottage
The Only Teahouse at Kokhe Danda

2025
Discover Parbat
6 min read

Hidden beneath the famous viewpoint of Kokhe Danda in Parbat, Nepal, Aapa Cottage is more than just a mountain teahouse — it is the heart of the Kokhe Danda trekking experience.

Surrounded by panoramic Himalayan scenery and peaceful forests, this cozy mountain lodge offers trekkers warmth, comfort, local food, and unforgettable sunrise views in the middle of the Annapurna region. Whether you are planning the Kokhe Danda Trek for photography, relaxation, or Himalayan views, here is everything you should know before staying at Aapa Cottage.

Aapa Cottage · Kokhe Danda, Parbat · ~3,000m

Because it is the only teahouse in the area, almost every trekker staying overnight at Kokhe Danda experiences the hospitality of this mountain lodge.

Location & Setting

Situated at around 3,000 metres elevation, Aapa Cottage lies just below the main Kokhe Danda viewpoint. The teahouse is built with a mix of wooden and concrete structure, giving it a traditional Himalayan atmosphere while still providing basic comfort for trekkers. From the dining hall itself, guests can enjoy stunning views of the Annapurna and Manaslu ranges, along with magical sunrise colours over the Himalayas.

Accommodation

Despite its remote mountain location, Aapa Cottage can accommodate a surprisingly good number of trekkers across multiple room types.

🏠 First Floor
5 twin-sharing rooms — comfortable and warm for couples or small groups trekking together.
🏠 Second Floor
2 hall rooms accommodating 5–6 people each, plus 4 additional twin-sharing rooms.

The rooms are simple but cozy, designed mainly for warmth and rest after a long trekking day.

The Warm Dining Hall

One of the highlights of Aapa Cottage is its welcoming dining hall. At the centre is a wood fire heater placed inside a cylindrical vessel, helping trekkers stay warm during cold evenings. After sunset, most trekkers gather here for food, tea, conversations, and mountain stories.

The Experience

The combination of fire warmth, Himalayan views, and peaceful mountain surroundings creates one of the most memorable moments of the Kokhe Danda Trek. It is the kind of evening you will not forget.

Sunrise & Mountain Views

One of the biggest reasons trekkers stay at Kokhe Danda is the incredible Himalayan panorama. From Aapa Cottage and the nearby viewpoint, you can witness the full sweep of the Annapurna range, the Dhaulagiri range, and the Manaslu range, all bathed in colourful sunrise light over snow-capped peaks.

Pro Tip

For the best experience, it is highly recommended to reach the teahouse before 5 PM. This gives you enough time to enjoy sunset views, relax properly, and wake up early for sunrise photography — often the most stunning moment of the entire trek.

Electricity, Charging & WiFi

Since Kokhe Danda is a remote mountain destination, facilities are basic and eco-friendly.

☀️
Solar Power The teahouse runs mainly on solar panels. Every room and hall has light, but charging sockets are only available in the dining hall and can become crowded during peak season. Carrying a power bank is highly recommended.
📶
Free WiFi WiFi is available at the teahouse. However, internet may not work properly during bad weather and connectivity can be unstable. It's best to enjoy the mountains and disconnect for a while.
🚿
Washrooms & Showers Asian-style washrooms are available. Hot and cold showers are possible at an extra charge. Since water must be carried manually from a source nearly an hour below, trekkers are requested to use water responsibly.

Booking & Payment Tips

📅
Book in Advance During autumn trekking season, spring season, festivals, and weekends, the teahouse can become fully occupied. Because Aapa Cottage is the only accommodation at Kokhe Danda, booking a few days ahead during busy periods is strongly recommended.
💵
Cash is King Due to unstable internet and remote mountain conditions, online payments may not always work and mobile banking can fail. Always carry enough Nepali cash before starting the trek.

Aapa Cottage may be simple, but it perfectly represents the beauty of Himalayan trekking culture.

With warm meals, cozy rooms, incredible mountain views, and genuine hospitality, it offers everything needed for an unforgettable stay at Kokhe Danda.

Arrive before 5 PM — stay for both the sunset and the sunrise.
